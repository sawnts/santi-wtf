---
title: creative blocks aren’t optional
description: creative blocks as seasons — winter, spring, summer, and autumn — and why the quiet one is where the deepest work happens.
date: 2026-05-07
---

for most of 2026, i haven’t made much. the ideas i reach for stay out of reach. the little work that does come out doesn’t feel right. a younger me would’ve spiraled — questioned his taste, his skills, whether the work was worth making at all.

the questions still come. i just don’t believe their stories anymore.

what we call a creative block is almost always a season. and seasons aren’t a sign that something is wrong. they’re proof the system is working as designed.

the carbon cycle, the nitrogen cycle, the water cycle — none of them move in straight lines. matter disappears from one form and re-emerges in another. nothing is wasted. nothing is lost. matter just changes state.

the creative process works the same way. it moves in seasons, and the one most of us fear — the quiet one — is where the deepest work happens.

## winter

the fallow season, when things go underground. your brain’s default mode network keeps firing below the surface, making connections the conscious mind can’t force. on the surface, it looks like nothing is happening. everything is.

## spring

gathering, absorbing, wandering, hunting. collecting raw material with no obvious purpose and trusting that some of it will be useful later.

## summer

execution. the idea breaks the surface and you make the work visible. work that carries the weight of everything that came before it.

## autumn

harvest and release. finishing, judging, letting go. the decay of what’s done becomes the soil for what’s next.

the mistake isn’t entering winter. the mistake is not recognizing it for what it is — and either forcing summer before it’s time, or believing summer will never come again.

i’ve been here before and, before i know it, i’ll be here again.
